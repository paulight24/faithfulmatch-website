Faith and purpose, at the center

FaithfulMatch exists to support people seeking relationships grounded in Christian faith, shared values and purpose. We can't promise that any match is divinely guaranteed, or that a relationship will succeed — but we've built the app to help you meet people who are looking for the same kind of intentional, faith-centered connection you are.

Frequently asked questions

What is FaithfulMatch?

FaithfulMatch is a Christian dating and relationship app that helps believers connect based on faith, values and shared purpose.

Who can use FaithfulMatch?

FaithfulMatch is for adults 18 and older who are looking for a faith-centered relationship.

Is FaithfulMatch only for Christians?

FaithfulMatch is built around Christian faith, denomination and church-involvement preferences, and is designed for people seeking a faith-centered relationship in that context.

How does matching work?

You set your preferences and faith details, then discover profiles that fit. When you and another member both express interest, you're matched and can start messaging.

Is FaithfulMatch available on Android and iPhone?

FaithfulMatch is not yet publicly available on Google Play or the Apple App Store. We're finishing store review preparation — join the launch list below to be notified.

How is my location displayed?

Other members only ever see an approximate distance (for example, "5 miles away"). Your precise location is never shown to other users.

How do I report or block someone?

You can report or block any user directly from their profile or from within a conversation. See our Safety page for details.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from Settings inside the app, or by contacting support. Full instructions are on our Account Deletion page.

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@faithfulmatch.love, or visit our Support page.
