Community Guidelines

FaithfulMatch is built to be a respectful, faith-centered community. These guidelines apply to every profile, photo, and message.

Not allowed on FaithfulMatch

  • Harassment of any kind
  • Threats of violence or harm
  • Hate speech, based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic
  • Nudity or sexually explicit material
  • Sexual exploitation of any kind
  • Content involving minors, in any context
  • Scams or fraudulent behavior
  • Soliciting money from other members
  • Impersonating another person or organization
  • Spam or repetitive unwanted contact
  • Commercial promotion or advertising unrelated to genuine dating intent
  • Illegal activity of any kind
  • Sharing another person's private information without their consent
  • Graphic violence
  • Manipulative, coercive, or abusive behavior toward other members

Enforcement

If you violate these guidelines, we may take action depending on severity, including:

  • Removing the content in question
  • Issuing a warning
  • Restricting certain features on your account
  • Temporarily suspending your account
  • Permanently removing your account
  • Cooperating with law enforcement or legal authorities where required by law

Report a violation

You can report a profile, photo, or message directly from within the app. See our Safety page for tips on staying safe, or contact support@faithfulmatch.love for anything these guidelines don't cover.
