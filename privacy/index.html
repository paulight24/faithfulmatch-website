Privacy Policy

Effective date: TBD — confirm before publishing · Last updated: TBD — confirm before publishing

FaithfulMatch is a service of Excellent Vision Real Estate, LLC ("we", "us", "our"), the legal operator of the FaithfulMatch app and website. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. FaithfulMatch is intended for users 18 years of age and older.

1. Information we collect

When you create and use a FaithfulMatch account, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and account credentials
  • Birth date and age
  • Gender
  • Dating and matching preferences
  • Christian faith information, including denomination, faith level, church involvement, and marriage intent
  • Spiritual interests and values you choose to share on your profile
  • Profile biography text you write
  • Profile photographs you upload
  • Approximate location, used to show distance between members
  • Precise device location, only if and when you separately grant location permission for a feature that requires it — see Section 3 for how location is actually used today
  • Likes, passes, matches, and other interaction activity within the app
  • Private messages you send and receive, and related message metadata (timestamps, delivery status)
  • Reports you submit, and records of accounts you block or that block you
  • Device identifiers, IP address, and application/error logs
  • Analytics data about how you use the app
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Subscription and purchase information (for example, whether you have an active Premium subscription)
  • Push notification tokens, used to deliver notifications to your device
  • Camera access, when you choose to take a profile photo directly in the app
  • Photo library access, when you choose to upload an existing photo

2. AI-assisted features

FaithfulMatch offers optional AI-assisted features, including conversation reply suggestions and profile/photo feedback. When you use these features, the relevant content (such as a conversation excerpt, your profile bio, or a photo) may be transmitted to a third-party AI provider in order to generate a suggestion. You always choose whether to use or send any AI-generated suggestion — the AI does not send messages on your behalf.

Technical detail requiring confirmation — the specific AI provider(s) actively processing user content in production must be confirmed and named here before publishing (see the internal review notes accompanying this site).

3. Location information

FaithfulMatch uses your approximate location to show other members roughly how far away you are (for example, "5 miles away") and to apply distance-based filtering to who you see in discovery. We do not display your precise location to other users. If precise location is ever collected for a specific feature, we will update this section to describe that collection accurately before the feature is released.

4. How we use information

  • To create and operate your account
  • To operate matching, messaging, and discovery features
  • To process subscription payments and manage billing
  • To send push notifications you have opted into
  • To investigate reports, enforce our Community Guidelines, and maintain platform safety
  • To monitor app performance, diagnose crashes, and improve the product
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with the following categories of service providers, strictly to operate the app:

  • Payment processing: Stripe, to process subscription payments and manage billing
  • AI providers: for optional AI-assisted conversation and profile-review features, as described in Section 2
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (GA4), to understand app usage
  • Authentication: Facebook Login (Meta), if you choose to sign in with Facebook
  • Hosting and infrastructure: our application hosting and database providers
Technical details requiring confirmation — see the separate internal review file accompanying this site for the full list of vendors, hosting providers, and practices that must be verified before this policy is published.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, in which case user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a successor policy.

6. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward as needed for fraud prevention, legal compliance, payment records, dispute resolution, or safety investigations. Specific retention periods are TBD and must be confirmed before publishing.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you reside, including the United States, where our service providers operate.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. You can delete your account at any time — see our Account Deletion page for instructions. To exercise any other privacy right, contact us at support@faithfulmatch.love.

10. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under California privacy law, including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request deletion. Contact us using the information below to exercise these rights.

11. Children's privacy

FaithfulMatch is restricted to users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will take steps to delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Material changes will be communicated through the app or by other reasonable means.
